The “DROP IT, HERMAN” era

As a tiny puppy, Herman treated downtown like a tasting menu: pick something up, wait for Jenny to notice, spit it out, repeat. Coincidence? Herman declines to comment.

Jenny: “Herman! DROP IT.”
Herman: “Excellent. Mom is looking at me again.”

Mom detected.

When Jenny gets home from work, Herman doesn't simply greet her. He becomes a kangaroo. And after the celebration? His personal chef still hand-feeds him. Some people own dogs. Jenny apparently runs a boutique restaurant for one very spoiled customer.

He loves everybody. But Jenny is home. ♥

THE JENNY FILES · INCIDENT #001

The Basketball Incident 🏀

Ball gets loose. I stand there. Jenny's brain: HE'S GOING FOR IT. Jenny takes off. I am still standing there. Jenny slips. Pinky mildly offended. Me: “...what just happened?”

Every once in a while, underneath all that planning, Jenny pulls out an absolutely wild move nobody saw coming.

Sometimes Plan A, B & C have their advantages.

Detroit. Chicago. Minneapolis. Every trip somehow better than the last. And on the way to Minneapolis, Jenny used her planning powers to locate something increasingly rare: a functioning Quiznos.

She knew I loved it. So she found one. It's little things like that that I remember.
